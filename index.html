Not just another reminder app

A calendar reminder is rigid. Hey You works differently: everyone you care about sits in a kind of lottery, with weights you can set, and each day you'll only ever be reminded to reach out to one person.

How it works

1

2

3

You reach out, mark it done, repeat.
